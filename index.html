1. Revenue Protection & Renewal Analytics

An estimated 35–40% revenue leakage was identified due to limited renewal data visibility across Great Plains, Salesforce, and license servers. Centralizing renewals data and automating the synchronization is a strategic revenue protection play with immediate ARR impact.

3. Architectural Risk & ERP Flexibility

Eliminating Technical Debt (~47 Point-to-Point Connections)

Vispero currently operates approximately 47 undocumented point-to-point integrations created by legacy contractors. Replacing these connections with a governed MuleSoft integration layer de-risks operations, establishes recovery SLAs, and enables scalable growth.

Future-Proofing ERP Agility ("Swappable Fitting")

Standardizing CRM-to-ERP integration rather than hard-coding legacy connections ensures Vispero remains ERP-agnostic. If the board evaluates replacing Great Plains in the future, the integration architecture remains intact—build once, adapt cheaply.
