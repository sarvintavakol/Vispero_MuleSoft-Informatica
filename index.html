1. Revenue Protection & Software Maintenance (SMA) Sync

Addressing the 35–40% SMA Renewal Leakage

An estimated 35–40% of SMA (Software Maintenance Agreement) renewal opportunities are currently missed because license end dates are unpopulated or trapped across Great Plains, Exact ERP (Europe), and ILM (License Server). Centralizing this data creates an automated engine that notifies customers when software "phones home."

Mandated Operational Fix: Nov 1, 2026 Mass Update

To immediately shake the tree, leadership authorized a mass update defaulting missing SMA end dates to November 1, 2026. Informatica and MuleSoft provide the permanent governance layer so future entitlements automatically populate in Salesforce without requiring manual intervention.

2. Admin Headcount Constraints & Support Upsell

Relieving Salesforce Admin Bottlenecks Under Hiring Freeze

A single Salesforce admin (Alina) is managing a major revenue platform while being swamped with routine enhancement requests. Because permanent headcount is constrained by a company-wide hiring freeze, automated data synchronization acts as a "surge resource"—freeing admin capacity for strategic projects.

Empowering Support Console Engagement

Vispero's support team—comprising blind and low-vision agents using the product live—currently lacks visibility into contract end dates during support calls. Pushing the "Golden Record" into the Salesforce Service Console equips agents to identify upcoming renewals and AI upgrade opportunities in real time.
